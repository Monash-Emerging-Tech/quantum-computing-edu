# Glossary

## Basis (quantum basis / basis state)

A basis is a set of linearly independent states that span the space of all possible quantum states.

For a single qubit, the basis states are |0⟩ and |1⟩, meaning the quantum state of a qubit can be written as a superpossition of these basis states.

Further reading: https://www.quantum-inspire.com/kbase/qubit-basis-states/

## Circuit (quantum circuit)

A quantum circuit is a series of quantum gates applied to qubits to perform computation.

It is analogous to electical circuits, with unitary operations acting on qubits, rather than gates acting on wires.

Further reading: https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/quantum-circuits/introduction

## Eigenvalue

An eigenvalue is scalar λ for a given matrix A, such that:

Av = λv

Eigenvalues are fundamentally a concept from linear algebra, however in quantum computing they often correspond to measurement outcomes.

Further reading: https://mathforquantum.quantumtinkerer.tudelft.nl/6_eigenvectors_QM/

## Eigenvector

An eigenvector is a non-zero vector that only scales (does not rotate) when a transformation matrix A is applied:

Av = λv

Eigenvectors are fundamentally a concept from linear algebra, however in quantum computing they often correspond to definite measurement results.

Further reading: https://mathforquantum.quantumtinkerer.tudelft.nl/6_eigenvectors_QM/
