This is a test circuit. It exists to demonstrate the built-in gates and other attributes of a circuit.

Built-in gates include: Identity; Pauli-X, -Y, and -Z; Hadamard; and Swap. There are also barrier gates whose only purpose is to separate the circuit into sections.
